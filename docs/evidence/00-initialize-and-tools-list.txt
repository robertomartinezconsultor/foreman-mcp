Tools (11):
- get_day_briefing: Owner briefing for today: jobs on the schedule, tomorrow's unassigned work, leads past the response SLA, low stock and outbox status.
- list_jobs: List jobs with stage, crew and scheduled visit. Optional date filter (YYYY-MM-DD).
- get_job: Full detail of one job, including its field-update thread (the business's memory of that job).
- get_unanswered_leads: Leads still waiting for a first reply, longest wait first, with hours waiting and contact info.
- check_inventory: Inventory levels; flags items at or below their low-stock threshold.
- log_field_update: Record a field update from a worker against a job (progress, problem, material note). This is how the business remembers what the field reported.
- assign_crew: Assign a crew to a job (or reassign). Crew ids: crew-a, crew-b, crew-c; list with list_jobs output or get_day_briefing.
- queue_dispatch: Queue a dispatch message to a crew or the owner in the outbox. The outbox is the delivery queue the ops console shows; messages persist.
- record_lead_reply: Record that a lead got a reply: saves the reply text on the lead's thread and closes the follow-up clock. State persists across sessions.
- create_shopping_list: Create an approval-gated shopping list draft. Without a job: restock every low item up to its threshold. With a job id: cover that job's material shortfall. Nothing is purchased; the draft waits for the owner's approval.
- run_morning_dispatch: THE agentic flow: prepare tomorrow end to end. Detects gaps (unassigned crews, double-bookings, material shortfalls, leads past the response SLA), composes and queues a dispatch per crew plus an owner summary with ready-to-send lead replies, and drafts an approval-gated shopping list for shortfalls.
